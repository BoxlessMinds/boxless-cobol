The Reading Room

Everything you need to keep going after the museum closes. Each entry says why it's worth your time and how deep the water is.

Start here

  • This repo's lessonsBEGINNER Eight short programs that teach the language in order, plus examples and a small library with unit tests.
  • Open Mainframe Project: COBOL Programming CourseBEGINNER Free, hands-on and open source. Written for people who have never touched a mainframe.
  • GnuCOBOLBEGINNER The free compiler this repo uses. Runs on Linux, WSL, macOS and Windows.
  • Wikipedia: COBOLBEGINNER A solid overview of the history, features and criticism, with plenty of sources to follow.

Books

  • Michael Coughlan, Beginning COBOL for Programmers (Apress, 2014)WORKING The best bridge if you already know another language. Clear examples, modern style.
  • Murach's Mainframe COBOL (Murach)WORKING The classic "how it's done at work" book, with paired pages of explanation and code.
  • GnuCOBOL guides (Programmer's Guide and more, free)WORKING Everything GnuCOBOL can do, from the people who build it.

Tools

  • COBOL extension for VS Code (bitlang)BEGINNER Syntax highlighting and column guides for fixed format. Makes column 72 hard to miss.
  • IBM Z XploreWORKING Free access to a real mainframe, with challenges that take you from first login to COBOL and beyond.
  • ZoweWORKING Open-source tools for working with z/OS from a modern laptop and editor.

Reference

  • IBM Enterprise COBOL for z/OS documentationDEEP END The language reference and programming guide for the COBOL most businesses actually run.
  • ISO/IEC 1989:2023DEEP END The current COBOL standard. Paid, long and very precise. For settling arguments.
  • GnuCOBOL on SourceForgeDEEP END Source code, releases and the discussion forum.

History

  • Computer History MuseumBEGINNER A real museum (unlike this one), with material on Grace Hopper, COBOL and the machines of the era.
  • Naval History and Heritage CommandBEGINNER Search for "Grace Hopper" for her official biography and photographs.
  • Smithsonian: the 1947 logbook with the mothBEGINNER The original "first actual case of bug being found".
  • Jean Sammet, "The Early History of COBOL", in History of Programming Languages (ACM, 1978)WORKING The story told by someone who was in the room.
  • Jean Sammet, Programming Languages: History and Fundamentals (1969)DEEP END A survey of 1960s languages, COBOL included. A museum piece in its own right.
  • Reuters (2017): banks and their COBOL systemsBEGINNER The source of the estimates on the Vault's stat wall.

Community

  • r/cobolBEGINNER Questions, war stories and job talk.
  • Open Mainframe ProjectBEGINNER Home of the COBOL course and the COBOL working group.
  • Stack Overflow: [cobol]WORKING Search here first when the compiler says something you don't understand.
